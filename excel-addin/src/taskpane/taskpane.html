Sign in to the Précis instance that generated the export.

Other Précis regions are not converted.

What changes?

The column headers, report and context footer become live, starting at the existing column-header row. Your title stays above the report and remains unchanged. Column headers are unmerged. Fresh results may add, remove or reorder data rows, so fixed-cell references may point to different lines.

For extract layout, Excel removes the Table and turns structured references into fixed cell references. Generated value-column names follow the spill.

Custom totals, calculated columns and slicers are unsupported.
